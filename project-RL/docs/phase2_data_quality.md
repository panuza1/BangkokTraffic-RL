# Phase 2 data quality and proposed split — 6 November 2026

**Decision: Bangkok-informed scenario only.** Licensed observations exist, but no SUMO network or calibration has been built or validated. Detector coverage and arrival flows, turning proportions, signal clearance assumptions, and independent queue/travel-time targets are not yet established. These data alone do not justify a claim of a calibrated Bangkok simulation.

## Missing-count policy

The five-second loop measurements are the only observations used below. For each approach, a 15-minute sensor sum is reported only if every constituent sensor count is present; incomplete intervals are blank and include their missing sensor-sample count. Do not use zero as a replacement or interpolate across an outage. No imputed or estimated values are produced; separate fields for each remain blank. Do not use incomplete intervals as complete demand. Repeated gaps, especially those shared across sensors, can reflect shared collection or processing problems; the data do not establish a cause. No duration is automatically safe to impute. Any future imputation must be separately labeled, justified against held-out masked observations, compared against the no-imputation case, and never use information from validation/test days to fit a training imputer.

`scripts/analyze_phase2.py` generates `data/processed/count_gaps.csv`: every missing-count run per date and approach with start inclusive, end exclusive, number of five-second timestamps, duration in seconds and missing **sensor-samples**. A run is consecutive timestamps where at least one sensor is missing; this is a union across an approach, not proof of an outage of every sensor. Its `phase2_manifest.json` gives per-approach total missing sensor-samples, unique timestamps, gap-length histograms and input hashes. Complete timestamps, including every individual gap, can be inspected in the CSV. The scan rejects missing/off-grid/duplicate timestamps and invalid numeric ranges; it does not infer downtime from valid zero counts.

## Candidate split and profiles — review required

The proposed **chronological** day split is Jun 2 training/calibration, Jun 3 validation, Jun 9 held-out test. Selection uses availability and missingness, not controller results. Jun 6 is an unassigned alternative; Jun 7 has no signal file and Jun 8 has a recorded morning connection loss. Three days are not evidence of broad generalization. This is a proposal, not a frozen final-test protocol: inspect and approve the split before any training, calibration or evaluation, then freeze it before using held-out outcomes.

All approaches have complete counts on all three proposed days for 07:00–07:15 and 09:00–09:30. Signal events occur on all three days in these windows, but boundary state and continuity have not been proven. These short, disjoint windows are candidate observations, not necessarily adequate episodes or warm-up periods; do not concatenate them as continuous demand. The stored profile `data/processed/candidate_profiles_15min.csv` has all 96 intervals per date and approach, with proposed split, complete observed vehicle count per 15 minutes, missing count, completeness percentage, status, and blank imputed/estimated columns. Completeness is 100 × present five-second volume sensor-samples ÷ (180 × number of sensors), rounded to six decimals; it describes data availability, not how much traffic was detected. The manifest `data/processed/phase2_manifest.json` records the sensor groups and CSV SHA-256. It contains no generated SUMO routes, arrival rates or turning ratios. Estimated arrival demand, if later derived, must be separate from measured stop-line counts, account for upstream queues and detector coverage, and carry explicit assumptions and sensitivity tests.

Original loop sensor grouping follows the authors' notebook; we assume distinct detector streams for summation, and have not mapped sensors to individual network lanes. Five-second values are vehicle counts; 15-minute sums are vehicles per 15 minutes, not PCU/h or arrivals/hour. Source timestamps are explicitly offset `+07:00` under the unverified local-time assumption. Raw and generated data are Git-ignored; manifest SHA-256 hashes support reproduction.
